# Changelog

Notable changes for Mingleza are documented here.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-21

### Added

- Email-and-password authentication, account recovery and callback handling, with adults-only (18+) onboarding and private account details.
- Member discovery and social interactions, including follows, friend requests, message requests, profile interactions and group conversations.
- Realtime conversation updates using Supabase Realtime.
- Monthly Premium (KES 200) and VIP (KES 1,000) membership flows, with Flutterwave card billing and M-Pesa payment support.
- Opt-in phone-number visibility controls for eligible Premium/VIP members.
- Gift diamonds: purchase at KES 1 per diamond, and request redemption at KES 0.80 per diamond with a 200-diamond minimum; Gift Card and PayPal redemption methods are offered.
- Supporting database migrations, access-control policies, server-side handlers, UI routes, tests and provider/deployment documentation for the new functionality.
