# Deferred work

## Implemented product baseline

The app uses email/password registration, sign-in and recovery, with age preflight and private registration details; existing profile IDs and onboarding are preserved. Supabase provider setup and controlled non-production validation remain pending; see `docs/PHASE-2.md`. Other implemented features include adult verification, validated profile setup, Supabase-backed profiles and discovery, friend requests, and direct messages between accepted friends. The social-interactions work adds follows, shareable profiles, recipient-approved message requests, friend-only group chats, and five-second polling of open conversations; polling is not instant realtime, and video-chat requests are invitations only. Premium/VIP membership, consent-gated phone visibility, payment verification/webhook handling, and card/M-Pesa checkout code exist but depend on configured providers and operational/legal readiness. Gifts & diamonds provide wallet/gift-transfer/redemption flows; purchase checkout also depends on payment-provider setup. Incomplete profiles remain out of Discover; friend and pending-request relationships are excluded. Profile photos use the existing profile-avatar storage bucket with an initials fallback. Explore is a privacy-safe coming-soon state rather than a demo map.

Logged-out visitors see the public marketing homepage and a searchable, categorized FAQ at `/faq`; signed-in, age-verified members continue to see Discover at `/`. Public `/terms` and `/privacy` pages are clearly marked draft placeholders pending legal review. The public sitemap lists `/`, `/faq`, `/terms`, and `/privacy`; application and member-profile routes remain excluded from crawler discovery. The `/likes` destination shows incoming dating likes and mutual matches; `/matches` is reserved for incoming and outgoing friendship requests. `/notifications` is the single notification inbox. Likes and Notifications are in the shared member navigation, not duplicated in page headers. Active mutual matches can use persisted dating chat at `/chat/$matchId`; match and dating-message events create member-scoped in-app notifications at `/notifications`.

The app stores only a derived adult-verification result and check time; it does not store a date of birth or birth year and does not display member ages. The current age check is self-attestation, not identity-document or biometric verification. Do not add age storage or display without an approved product decision.
